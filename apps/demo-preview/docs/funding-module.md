## Calculation

Annual work quantity = relevant asset quantity × annual frequency. Mowing with the selected2m-wide method converts m²/2000 to pass-km. Patching forecast is already annual and is not multiplied again. One-off repairs always use measured defect quantity once. Whole-luminaire replacement and element straightening require explicit method classification. Damaged assets without a supported repair do not disappear behind routine cleaning; missing repair scope is flagged.

Manual work selection requires a separate work quantity and unit. It does not automatically repair the entire asset. A manual work band replaces that asset's automatic bands; users should split separately managed work scopes in their RAMS export when multiple manual activities are planned.

Workers/operators = ceil(total selected-road role hours / approved annual productive hours), rounded once across the selected organization. The full annual payroll is charged once; hourly wages are not added again. Monthly pay = base×coefficient×(1+allowance%) + monthly meals + annual holiday payment/12. Payroll is allocated to work/roads by role hours. Shared costs are allocated to roads by direct-cost share; if direct cost is zero, length is used. Allocation starts from the rounded grand total, caps each rounded road amount at the remaining balance, and gives the final positive-cost road that balance. A zero-cost road never receives a negative rounding remainder. The workbook uses the same rule. Empty selected-road scopes cannot be saved or exported. Missing-data counts are scoped by road ID, including asset-level gaps outside valid work lines.

Employer social cost applies to payroll. Overhead applies to payroll+materials+machinery+PPE+employer social cost+explicit other cost. Contingency applies to that subtotal+overhead. All rates are user-entered organization assumptions; default prices/hours are labelled demo, tax/overhead default0 are not statutory claims. Use final tax-inclusive material/machinery rates. Employee deductions are not extra employer expense.

Missing resource recipes, prices, operator labor, units or source decisions produce `complete=false`. The prominent total is the known subtotal, not a full appropriation. Default demo has deliberately unresolved sign/lighting resource recipes rather than fabricated water or machinery consumption. A chief may supply a technology-card recipe with provenance.

## RAMS connector

`GET /api/funding/status` returns configuration status. `GET /api/funding/assets` fetches a fixed administrator-configured HTTPS endpoint in `RAMS_ASSETS_URL`; optional server secret `RAMS_API_TOKEN` supplies Bearer authorization. Keys never go to the browser. The Site's authenticated-user header is required; cross-site requests and redirects are rejected. Upstream response is bounded to5MB and15s and must pass `parseAssetSnapshot`. Failures preserve the current user calculation, never masquerade as live demo data.

Payload: schemaVersion1, source, observedAt YYYY-MM-DD, roads[]. Road: unique id, code/name, importance INTERNATIONAL/STATE/LOCAL, category Ia/Ib/II/III/IV/V, lengthKm, carriagewayWidthM, condition, optional trafficPerDay, assets[]. Asset: unique ID within road, kind PAVEMENT/GRASS/DRAIN/CULVERT/SIGN/PAVILION/BARRIER/CURB/LIGHTING/OTHER, name, quantity, unit, condition. Optional measured defectQuantity, industrialZone, repairMethod STRAIGHTEN/REPLACE_FIXTURE, pavement parameters thicknessMm/largestPatchM2/removeOld, workId/workQuantity/workUnit, annualFrequency/frequencyBasis, resourcePlan, operatorHoursPerUnit/operatorBasis. Maximum100 roads and5000 assets. Duplicate IDs, invalid numbers/dates/units fail clearly.

JSON import is functional now. The Site can export its current snapshot as a format example. Importing a saved budget JSON restores snapshot+policy; data is labelled imported. The current Supabase project was read-only checked and is INACTIVE; no schema or credentials were changed. Notion/Slack RAMS searches returned no integration contract. Live RAMS URL/token remain unconfigured; native integration has been contract-tested with bounded mocked responses only.

## Saving and export

Versioned localStorage (`roadops-funding-v1`) retains up to10 explicitly saved snapshots, policy sets and optional budget limits in that browser; it is not a shared server database. Older saved records without a limit still open. Partial resource cards are preserved as drafts through JSON round-trip. JSON preserves the editable model for transfer. Existing payroll/act Excel formats are unchanged.

The budget now downloads a real `.xlsx` with eight worksheets: Jamlama, Yo‘llar, Ish haqi, Ishlar, Resurslar, Jihozlar, Texnika parki and Hisob shartlari. `workbook-model.ts` owns values, formula dependencies and layout; `export-workbook.ts` fills the styles authored with Artifact Tool and retained in `workbook-template.json`. The runtime download is lazy-loaded and needs no external export service.

The first sheet shows the known total, expense shares, scope and limit/shortfall/surplus. Detailed rows preserve numbers as numbers, blank missing prices, source references and export-time limitations. Typed literal text cannot become an Excel formula. Financial inputs are blue; referenced calculations are green in working schedules, and totals have a distinct style. Rate edits recalculate payroll, resource cost, PPE and road allocations. Asset quantities, labor requirements, source decisions and export-time missing-data notes remain the exported planning snapshot and must be refreshed in the Site. Excel edits do not sync back automatically.

The file includes filters, frozen row/road labels, data validation, repeated print headers and A4/A3 landscape print areas; very wide schedules use two pages across to retain readable text. Summary differences are independent review checks, never inputs to the budget calculation. Missing and zero inputs are distinguished with COUNT guards. Budget limit is saved with the scenario and included in the workbook, with any unallocated surplus displayed.

To regenerate the authored style asset, compile `workbook-model.ts`/`assets.ts` as `funding-model.mjs`/`assets.mjs` into an OS temporary directory. Copy `scripts/build-funding-template.mjs` there, symlink the Codex primary runtime dependencies there, and run with the primary runtime Node. Then use `scripts/extract-funding-template.py` to read the XLSX styles into the Site template. No alternate workbook-authoring libraries are required. The shared model keeps runtime output and the authored workbook aligned.

## Approved budget, annual allocation and execution (September 2026)

The budget approval path now creates the annual program directly. The approved source snapshot, policy, exact IQN work IDs, resource assessment, selected range norm, road IDs, units and annual quantities are retained. Repeating the same approval is idempotent. A changed budget creates an archived revision; committed quantities cannot be removed or reduced, and an executing line's recipe or per-unit labor norms cannot change. A deficient funding limit prevents approval of the larger scope.

Coverage review contains all 29 TIME domains, 50 physical Annex 1 rows/branches covering 45 numbered requirements, and IQN 03 staffing/pay, equipment, transport, fleet and other-expense scopes. Each road scope needs linked costed works or a reasoned exclusion. RESOURCE works are explicitly assigned to their maintenance domain by the reviewer; the resource-series topic is not falsely treated as a TIME domain. All costed lines require a domain link. The 97 unresolved catalog rows are not invented as zero norms. Valid range rows accept a bounded selection using the source basis quantity. Missing source values, incompatible units, unresolved resources and absent tariffs block approval. Diagnostics/audit total rows and component rows cannot be charged together. The supplied source has no Annex 2 heading; none was manufactured.

Monthly allocations start unassigned. The chief may enter seasonal quantities or deliberately distribute the annual quantity equally. Sum(monthly allocations) must not exceed the approved annual quantity. Each month and year also enforce verified actual quantity plus outstanding reserved quantity against the corresponding limit. Preview, publication (including all jobs together), rescheduling and monthly edits check these constraints. Multi-month job inputs are split by the user into separate monthly orders. Defect work in an approved-budget workflow must match a budget line; an unapproved destination year cannot bypass control. Legacy outstanding orders must finish or be cancelled before the first budget approval for their year.

Assigned, started and pending-verification work reserves its full planned quantity. Chief verification credits only actual quantity, releasing the unused remainder. Cancelling an unstarted order releases volume, material reservations and equipment occupancy. Verified work cannot be cancelled. Physical line completion is verified quantity / approved quantity. Overall progress uses frozen normative labor hours as weights; if all weights are zero it uses the mean of line percentages. Actual overtime cannot inflate progress. The annual XLSX includes both yearly and monthly/YTD figures (IQN 03-24 Annex V).

## Full Table 3 equipment and staffing

All 44 IQN 03-24 Table 3 rows are represented from the original PDF. Personal issue eligibility uses its eight occupation columns. Physical people and payroll FTE are separate: a half-time employee still counts as one eligible person. Support staff and their pay require explicit organization-approved counts and sources. A source '+' is eligibility, not an asserted quantity of one; the per-person calculation assumption is editable and reviewed.

Personal quantities use eligible heads; department tools use department count; temporary signs use worksite count. Soap is 0.2 kg/person/month. Items with a stated lifetime use annual financial provision, not a fabricated purchase schedule. Cones (15/department) and temporary signs (8/worksite) have no source lifetime: their initial requirement less usable stock is costed once. Paint, lime, asphalt, cement and metal appear as work-derived materials and are not added again as personal equipment. Worker cards expose all applicable source-backed lifetime norms, preserve issue-date timers, and accept the correct kg/m units for consumables. No service lifetime is invented for the rows where the source omits it.
